# Original prayer tracker illustration assets

All original SVG artwork in this directory was authored from original vector shapes for this application. The two generated PNG replacements preserve the same child character and were created for improved thumbs-up and hero rendering. There are no downloaded illustration assets, stock images, third-party logos, external SVG resources, or quoted religious text.

- `child-waiting.svg`, `child-home.svg`, `child-mosque.svg`: the original consistent child character in three prayer states, 260 × 260
- `child-home.png`: generated replacement matching the child character, with one correct thumbs-up thumb and four curled fingers
- `child-mosque.png`: generated replacement matching the child character, with a blue hero outfit, red cape, and raised fist with a correctly folded thumb
- `header.svg`: mosque and garden panorama, 1400 × 300
- `fajr.svg`, `dhuhr.svg`, `asr.svg`, `maghrib.svg`, `isha.svg`: time-of-day mosque landscapes, 400 × 160

Characters use matching face, cap, outline, color and shading conventions; landscapes share original mosque, palm and garden shape language. SVGs can be scaled without loss of clarity.
